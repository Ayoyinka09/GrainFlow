### **User**

The main users are **warehouse staff, factory team, and the logistics department** who need to coordinate the movement of goods from supplier to warehouse to factory for cleaning and bagging back to the warehouse and ultimately to the millers and give account of production losses..

### **Problem**

Grain cleaners can struggle to maintain accurate, real-time visibility of **inventory, warehouse activities, production receipts, and deliveries** when these processes are managed in silo. This can lead to stock discrepancies, delayed order fulfilment, poor coordination between the factory and warehouse, and inefficient delivery planning.

### **Main journey**

The user should be able to **receive goods from the factory, record and track inventory in the warehouse, manage stock movements and orders, prepare goods for dispatch, and coordinate deliveries**. The system should provide real-time visibility across the warehouse, factory, and delivery processes so that each team knows what has been produced, what is available in stock, what is being dispatched, and what has been delivered.
